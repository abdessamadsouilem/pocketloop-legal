Wordly Loop – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

Wordly Loop (package app.pocketloop.wordlyloop) is published by Pocketloop, operated by Abdessamad Souilem ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with data, and what it does not do. Short version: the app's own features keep your data on your device; the only data that leaves your phone is what Google's advertising, billing and Play services need to work, and you can turn advertising off by purchasing Remove Ads.

1. Data the app itself handles (stays on your device)

  • Game progress (current level, coins, daily puzzle state, streak, settings) is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. "Reset progress" in Settings erases it; so does uninstalling.
  • Sharing. "Challenge a friend" sends a text you can see beforehand, only to the app you pick in Android's share sheet.
  • Wordly Loop needs no account and works offline.

We do not run our own servers for this app. We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, or precise location.

The app contains software development kits (SDKs) from Google. They collect data as described below and in their own policies, which we encourage you to read.

3. Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regulated regions). On first launch the app shows Google's consent form. You can change your choices at any time from Settings → Ad privacy settings inside the app.
  • Personalised ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of personalised ads in Android: Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  • Remove Ads. Purchasing Remove Ads in the app's Settings disables the advertising SDK for that installation, so no advertising data is collected from then on.
  • Delete your data. All of the app's own data is on your device: clear it from the app's settings or by uninstalling. For data held by Google, use Google's Ad Center and your Google Account.

4. Security and retention

Data sent to the Google services above is encrypted in transit. The app's own data is stored in Android's app-private storage and is removed when you uninstall. We keep nothing on servers because we operate none.

5. Children

Wordly Loop is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help you remove any data.

6. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, note it in the app's store listing. The current version is always available at this address.
